# Resonant circuits

A circuit containing both a *capacitor* and *inductor*.

For any such circuit, there is a frequency where the inductive and capacitive reactances are the same, that is $X_L = X_C$  where $Z = R +jX$ .
> This frequency is the resonant frequency

$$
\begin{align}
X_L &= 2\pi fL = X_c = \frac{1}{2\pi fC} \\
f_{res} &= \frac{1}{2\pi \sqrt{LC}}
\end{align}
$$

*Broad* resonance means the resonance effect is much less discriminatory against other frequencies than a *sharp* one.
> Broad circuits are used when the apparatus must give about the same response over a band of frequencies, whereas sharp gives good [[selectivity]]
